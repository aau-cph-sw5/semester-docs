| `MET-C-003` | Answered | Metro | What does signing mean, and is there a regulatory standard? **"There is no regulatory standard the electronic equivalent must satisfy."** The signature sheets show what the act means in practice: each signatory initials to confirm having read and understood the restriction and its accompanying documents, and there are two signature moments, implementation and cancellation, recorded side by side for the same person.
